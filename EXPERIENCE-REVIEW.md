## 3. Proposed rewrites — the Esker arc

The four roles should tell one story: *executes work → owns delivery → leads on
the hardest accounts → owns architecture across projects*. Each level should
answer "what could this person do that they couldn't a year earlier?"

### Associate Development Consultant · Aug 2020 – Sep 2021

Deliberately the shortest list. Three sharp bullets read as a starting point;
six generic ones read as a thin senior role.

> - Delivered JavaScript customizations extending Esker's Order Management
>   platform, building to designs set by senior consultants across concurrent
>   customer implementations.
> - Built REST-based integrations connecting customer ERP systems to the
>   platform, extending the team's established patterns to new customer
>   requirements.
> - Wrote internal tooling that removed repeated setup work from later
>   projects.

### Development Consultant · Sep 2021 – Apr 2024

The step up is **ownership** — you become the customer's technical contact, and
you start building things other people use.

> - Owned technical delivery on customer implementations end to end, from
>   requirements through go-live, as the customer's primary technical contact.
> - Extended the ERP integration catalogue well beyond standard connectors —
>   SAP ECC and S/4HANA, Oracle EBS and JDE, Microsoft D365, NetSuite, AS400,
>   QAD — including middleware routes via Boomi and MuleSoft where a direct
>   connection was out of scope.
> - Built and maintained the shared code repository the US development team
>   works from, cutting repeated build effort out of every later
>   implementation. [How many consultants draw on it?]
> - Contributed the technical solution design that helped win one of Esker's
>   largest accounts globally.
> - Established the US division's centralized documentation process for
>   knowledge transfer between consultants.

### Senior Development Consultant · Apr 2024 – Apr 2025

The step up is **difficulty and trust** — you get the accounts that can go
wrong, and you start being the architect rather than the builder. Every bullet
here is a real project of yours that is currently invisible on this page.

> - Took the accounts with the heaviest customization, including a
>   multi-account consolidation onto one of the organization's most customized
>   SAP ECC ordering portals, with complete order history preserved through
>   migration.
> - Acted as solution architect for the first production deployment of Esker's
>   new versioning infrastructure, working directly with R&D on framework
>   changes that shipped to every customer on the platform. It is now the
>   deployment path for all new Order Management projects.
> - Deployed to Malaysia on short notice to automate customer submissions to
>   the government's MyInvois portal, onboarding [N] customers ahead of the
>   national e-invoicing mandate.
> - Designed real-time REST integrations validating order pricing against
>   customers' own systems across stock, contract, customer-specific and
>   quote-based pricing.
> - Reviewed technical designs and mentored development consultants on
>   integration patterns. [Roughly how many?]

### Solution Architect · Apr 2025 – Present

The step up is **scope without authority** — you influence through architecture
and review rather than through reporting lines. Say that explicitly; it is a
harder skill than managing, and hiring managers know it.

> - Guide the technical direction of four development consultants across
>   concurrent customer projects, owning architecture and design review rather
>   than a reporting line.
> - Team delivered the highest billable utilization of any US team at 69.8%
>   through Q3 2026.
> - Led design of a configurable EDI 850 framework that lets customer
>   administrators onboard new trading partners themselves, removing a
>   developer dependency that previously sat with my team for every new
>   partner.
> - Designed the operating model for a new offshore delivery hub in Malaysia —
>   task handoff, documentation standards, communication cadences and quality
>   gates — so it could absorb development across simultaneous customer
>   projects.
> - Project-managed rollout of an internal Copilot-based development tool
>   across the Professional Services team, from stakeholder engagement through
>   adoption.
> - Partner with R&D on architecture decisions affecting the wider platform
>   rather than a single account.

Three notes on this role:

1. **Drop the hands-on coding bullet.** "Design, develop, test, and deploy
   customizations in JavaScript and TypeScript" is currently your second bullet
   here. It contradicts the story you are telling and repeats three other
   roles. The skills section already proves you can code.
2. **"Highest billable utilization at 69.8%"** is your best number, but only a
   consulting reader knows 69.8% is good. Consider "...at 69.8%, against a
   [target]% target" if you can cite one.
3. **"through Q3 of 2026"** will read as stale within months. Either drop the
   qualifier or plan to update it.
